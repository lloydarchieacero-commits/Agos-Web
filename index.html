FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about rural power, sensor durability, and species support.

AGOS was engineered in the Philippines specifically for rural farms. The controller runs 100% offline logic locally — aerators trigger immediately via industrial relays even without cellular signal. Solar-and-battery backup options are also available.
Standard installation takes less than 1 business day per pond cluster. We retrofit earthen ponds, HDPE-lined ponds, concrete raceways, and round tanks without draining water or disturbing active fish stocks.
Pre-calibrated profiles are included for Nile Tilapia, Bangus (Milkfish), Vannamei Shrimp, Sugpo (Black Tiger Prawn), and Pangasius. Custom alarm thresholds can also be set.
Unlike older galvanic probes, optical dissolved oxygen probes do not consume electrolyte or wear out delicate membranes. A quick rinse with water every 2 to 4 weeks during routine caretaker rounds is sufficient.
